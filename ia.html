Navigation map: before and after

Five top-level sections replace the dashboard-and-tiles funnel. Every existing feature stays, and is reachable in two clicks or fewer.

Before (funnel)
  • Dashboard
  • → Quick action: Browse jobs → Work Opportunities (Explore / Applications / Active Work / History)
  • → Quick action: Training → Catalog / My Learning / Saved
  • → Quick action: Assessments
  • → Quick action: Certificates
  • → Quick action: Investments
  • → Skills (Manage)
After (platform)
  • Home (feed)
  • Jobs browse + detail
  • My Work Applications · Interviews · Contracts · History
  • Learn Training · Catalog · Assessments · Certificates · Investments
  • Inbox Notifications · Messages · Interviews · Ask TIQ
  • Me Profile · Skills · Readiness · Account

Mobile behaviour

  • Top bar shrinks to logo and notification bell.
  • Bottom tab bar: Home, Jobs, Work, Learn, Me. Inbox is the bell; Ask TIQ is a floating button.
  • Three-column pages collapse to a single column: profile card and shortcuts move below the feed.
  • Jobs list and job detail become two separate screens.
