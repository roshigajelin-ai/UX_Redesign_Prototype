Comparison: current layout vs reference platforms

Design-only study for the freelancer experience. Dummy data. Written 11 Oct 2026.

Evidence note. The consumer-app repo (src/features/freelancer, src/app/App.tsx) was not available to the author of this page, so the current-state section is based on the live dashboard screens (Dashboard, Work Opportunities, Training, Assessments). Re-check route names against App.tsx before sign-off. The three NDT/energy sites were read on 11 Oct 2026. The general platforms are summarised from general knowledge and should be confirmed with screenshots.

4. Patterns we are borrowing

  • Persistent global nav with 5 sections (LinkedIn, Upwork). Replaces dashboard-as-hub.
  • Home feed with a “continue where you left off” card (Coursera) and matched jobs (Naukri, Upwork).
  • Profile strength meter that points to the next action (Naukri, LinkedIn).
  • Job alerts and follow (energyjobsearch) as a return trigger.
  • Verified credentials on the profile (Credly).
  • Bottom tab bar on mobile (energyjobsearch, LinkedIn).

Sample numbers in the mock (match %, “45 jobs”) are placeholders and must come from real job data when built.
