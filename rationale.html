Rationale

Why the freelancer experience moves from a dashboard funnel to a navigable platform.

Problem

Users land on the dashboard, use it, and leave. Features sit behind quick-action tiles, so they are hard to find and there is no reason to browse. It behaves like a form flow, not a platform.

What changes

  • Global nav on every page: Home, Jobs, My Work, Learn, Inbox, Me.
  • Home becomes a feed: continue-learning card, matched jobs, skill-gap nudge, reminders, news.
  • Related features are grouped: training, assessments, certificates and investments live together in Learn. Applications, interviews and contracts live together in My Work.
  • Features are linked to each other: a course says how many jobs need its skill; a job says which assessment it needs.
  • Ask TIQ is one click from anywhere.
